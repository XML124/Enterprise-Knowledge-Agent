Engineering Cooling System Manual
Model: ECS-200

Maximum operating temperature: 50 degrees Celsius.
Minimum operating temperature: 2 degrees Celsius.
Recommended nominal coolant pressure is 3.0 bar.
The system uses two variable-speed cooling pumps operating in duty/standby configuration.
If the duty pump becomes unavailable, the standby pump should begin operation within three seconds.
Scheduled maintenance should be performed every 750 operating hours.
