Engineering Cooling System Manual
Model: ECS-100

Maximum operating temperature: 45 degrees Celsius.
Minimum operating temperature: 5 degrees Celsius.
The system should not operate continuously above 42 degrees Celsius for more than 30 minutes.
Recommended nominal coolant pressure is 2.5 bar.
If coolant pressure falls below 1.8 bar, the controller should trigger a low-pressure warning.
The system includes two cooling pumps.
Pump A is the primary pump.
Pump B is configured as a redundant backup.
If Pump A fails, Pump B should automatically activate within five seconds.
Maintenance inspections should be performed every 500 operating hours.
The coolant filter should be inspected for blockage during each scheduled service.
